# Privacy Policy for Promptrix

**Last Updated:** April 30, 2026

Thank you for using Promptrix ("the Extension"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Chrome Extension. 

By using Promptrix, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
Promptrix is designed to act as your AI Memory. To provide this functionality, the Extension collects the following information when you interact with supported AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot):

*   **Chat Content:** The text of the prompts you send and the responses you receive from the AI.
*   **Metadata:** The name of the AI tool used, the URL/link of the specific chat session, and the date/time (timestamp) of the interaction.
*   **Token Estimates:** Estimated token counts for the captured prompts and responses.

**We do not collect:**
*   Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number, unless it is incidentally included within the text of your AI prompts.
*   Your login credentials or authentication tokens for the AI platforms.
*   Browsing history outside of the specific supported AI platform chat pages.

## 2. How We Handle and Use Your Data
The data collected by Promptrix is used exclusively to provide the core features of the Extension:

*   **Display and Organization:** To show your captured prompts and responses in your local and web-based dashboards.
*   **Search and Autocomplete:** To provide inline prompt suggestions as you type on supported AI platforms.
*   **Analytics:** To generate local usage statistics and visual graphs regarding your AI interactions.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training external AI models.

## 3. Data Storage
Promptrix gives you control over where your data is stored.

*   **Local Storage:** By default, all captured data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API. 
*   **Cloud Synchronization (Optional):** If you configure cloud syncing, your data may be securely transmitted to and stored in:
    *   **Your configured Cloudflare Worker:** To enable syncing across multiple devices or the standalone web dashboard.
    *   **Your GitHub Gist (Backup):** If you enable the GitHub backup feature, data is stored in your personal GitHub account.
*   The Extension relies on your own configured cloud endpoints. We do not maintain central servers or databases that store your prompt data.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
**We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties.** 

Your data remains entirely within your control—either stored locally on your machine or within the personal cloud services (Cloudflare/GitHub) that you explicitly configure.

## 5. Security
We value your trust in providing us with your AI interaction data. We use commercially acceptable means to protect it, including local extension storage mechanisms and standard HTTPS protocols when syncing to your configured cloud endpoints. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Your Rights and Control
*   **Deletion:** You can delete specific prompts, clear your entire database, or uninstall the Extension at any time to remove locally stored data.
*   **Cloud Data:** If you use the cloud sync features, you must manage data deletion directly within your Cloudflare Worker or GitHub Gist accounts.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support link provided in the Chrome Web Store.
